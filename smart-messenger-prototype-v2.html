How the score works
read_delay = read_at − delivered_at
read_speed = 1 ÷ (1 + avg_delay_min ÷ 10)
time_score = min(avg_min_per_day ÷ 22, 1)
score = 100 × (0.5·read_speed + 0.5·time_score)
22 min = your most-used chat. New contacts show “—” until 5 messages are tracked.
Example: Priya
read_speed = 1 ÷ (1 + 0.2) = 0.83
time_score = 20 ÷ 22 = 0.91
score = 100 × (0.415 + 0.455) = 87

Assumptions used in this wireframe
Q1 Unread chats first, then read. The habit score orders each group. A Recent toggle shows the plain newest-first order.
Q2 Read speed and time spent weigh 50 / 50.
Q3 One category per contact.
Q4 Groups go into the same categories (Product Team → Professional).
Q5 AI assist is optional. It opens only when you tap ✦ AI assist. It first asks what you want to say, then suggests replies.
Q6 Tasks can repeat, and tasks with no date are allowed.
Q7 If a voice task is unclear, the PA asks a follow-up question.
Q8 “My rules” covers ethics (avoid), etiquette (tone), and culture and language.
Q9 The AI can draft a scheduled message. You approve it once.
Q10 Recurring messages send the same text every time.
